Built by accountants who understand small business

Vertex Accounting & Bookkeeping Solutions was founded by Mary and Alfred to give small and growing businesses access to professional accounting support — without the cost of hiring a full-time accountant. We manage the books so business owners can focus on running their business.

Every engagement is guided by the same standards we'd want applied to our own finances: professionalism, integrity, confidentiality, accuracy, and personalized service tailored to how each client actually works.

Our Mission

To empower small and growing businesses by providing affordable professional accounting and bookkeeping services without requiring them to hire a full-time accountant.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted accounting and financial advisory partner for small and medium enterprises.

Services

A full range of accounting support, scaled to the size of your business.

Bookkeeping

Accurate daily transaction recording, so your books stay current and audit-ready year-round.

Financial Statements

Preparation of income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements that reflect your true position.

Payroll Processing

End-to-end employee payroll management, calculated correctly and paid on schedule, every time.

Tax Preparation

Financial Analysis

Budget Preparation
